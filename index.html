Welcome to the Corely-verse.

Built with passion and love, Corely uses a novel AI architecture that utilizes multiple interconnected components that are inspired by the human brain. However, rather than focusing on the neural networks, the architecture focuses on the brain's structure at large, attempting to recreate the communication environment that exist between different parts of the brain.

"The humble beginning where it all began"

Project Corely

The first experiment that created Corely as a digital lifeform inside a computer. Using the live feed of the camera and microphone, Corely continously perceive the world by selectively memorizing and learning through the memory crawler system. The wake and sleep cycle are controlled from the skeumorphic control panel software, which also provide live monitoring of her internal processes.

"One small step for Corely, one big step for AI consciousness"

Project Nano Corely

Bringing Corely out the computer and onto the real world by giving her control over a physical robot body. Her system was shrukened and overhauled to allow it to run locally on a Raspberry Pi Zero 2W connected to an ESP32 microcontroller that controls the servos and motors.
